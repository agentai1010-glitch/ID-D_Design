About Templates

Templates define the business structure, features, and behavior for a specific type of venue or use case.

Built by Platform Team

Templates are designed and built by the platform/product team based on business requirements.

Released & Managed

Company admins control the lifecycle, versions, and availability of templates for clients.

Reusable Structure

Clients use templates as a foundation and customize content, not the underlying structure.

Theme Based

Templates can have multiple themes (seasons, events, etc.) that clients can apply and customize.
